Swarm Intelligence Concepts
===========================

- Simple rules lead to complex, emergent behavior
- Agents are typically "memory-less" but act as a distributed system
- Properties: robustness, scalability, flexibility
- Comparison to ant colony optimization, BEECLUST, boid models
- Research references and further reading:

    - S. Camazine (2003), Self-Organization in Biological Systems
    - M. Dorigo, T. Stützle (2004), Ant Colony Optimization
